## Questions

### 1. The kick that never landed
K free kicks from its 40. The ball is **untouched by anyone in the field of play**, crosses the sideline in flight at R's 35, and is caught by R1 while R1 is **standing out of bounds**. Ruling?

### 2. Momentum, revisited
B1 intercepts a pass at his own 4-yard line and his momentum carries him into his end zone. B1 then runs **out** of the end zone to the 3, reverses field, circles **back into** the end zone, and is downed there. Ruling?

### 3. Two-point conversion, three ways to be wrong
On a try from B's 3, A1 fumbles. The ball is nearly at rest at B's 3-yard line when B1's muff is judged a new force, sending the ball into **B's own end zone**, where B2 recovers and (a) downs it, or (b) advances to B's 10. Ruling in each?

### 4. Post-scrimmage kick, wrong direction
K2 punts from the 50. The kick crosses the neutral zone, bounces at R's 45, and **rebounds backward** to K's 48, where R4 recovers. Prior to the end of the kick, R6 clipped K11 at **R's 40**. K accepts. Exact result?

### 5. The three-pointer that isn't
Following a safety and then a couple of dead-ball penalties against R, K is free kicking from **R's 40**. K1 place kicks the ball squarely between the uprights and over the crossbar. Ruling?

### 6. Numbers game
It is **second and 12**. Team A shifts into a scrimmage-kick formation and snaps with a player numbered 27 at snapper. How many A players numbered 50–79 must be on the line, and what is the snapper's eligibility status?

### 7. Punt formation disaster
Fourth and 15 from A's 8. A1 receives the snap **in his own end zone**, fumbles, recovers, and throws a forward pass to **ineligible** A2, who is also in the end zone. A2 muffs it and the pass falls incomplete. What are B's options and the result of each?

### 8. Bad box
The ball is on the 50, 10 to go. The down-marker erroneously reads fourth down when it is actually **third** down, and K punts. R6 returns the kick for an apparent touchdown. Before the try — and before the period ends — the error is discovered. Ruling?

### 9. No kickoff to carry it to
The fourth period ends during the down. Team A (a) scores a field goal that **ties** the game, or (b) scores a touchdown leaving A trailing by **one**. In both, B commits a live-ball foul during the scoring play. Where may A have the penalty enforced?

### 10. First touching, erased
Fourth and 5 from K's 30. K9 punts; K88 grounds and touches the kick (first touching) at R's 30. R35 picks it up at R's 25, is hit, and fumbles at R's 28; **K88 recovers** at R's 26. During the kick, R55 held. What are K's options?

## Answer Key

**1.** **Free kick out of bounds — a foul by K.** The kick did not end until it was touched out of bounds, so the ball was never legally touched inbounds by R. R may take a 5-yard penalty and re-kick, a 5-yard penalty from the succeeding spot, the ball at the inbounds spot 25 yards beyond the previous spot (R's 35), or the ball at the inbounds spot.
*Case 6.1.9 SIT B(d); Rule 6-1-9 PENALTY, 6-1-10*

**2.** **Safety.** Once B1 advanced out of the end zone, the momentum exception was cancelled; action thereafter dictates the result.
*Case 8.5.2 SIT F(b); Rule 8-5-2a EXCEPTION*

**3.** **One point for Team A in both (a) and (b).** The try ended when B2 recovered. Since in any ordinary down that dead ball in B's end zone would be a safety, A scores one point — and B's advance to the 10 is irrelevant, because only A may score during a try.
*Case 8.3.3 SIT A; Rules 4-2-2i, 8-1, 8-5-2b*

**4.** **R's ball, first and 10 at R's 25.** All PSK conditions are met, so the basic spot is the post-scrimmage kick spot — **K's 48**, where the kick ended. R6's foul at R's 40 is *behind* that spot, making it a **spot foul**: 15 yards from R's 40.
*Case 10.4.3 SIT E; Rules 2-41-6, 10-4-3*

**5.** **Touchback — no score.** A kickoff, *including the free kick following a safety*, cannot score a field goal, regardless of where dead-ball penalties have moved the free-kick line. R's ball, first and 10 from its 20.
*Case 8.4.1 SIT B(c); Table 6-4, Rules 8-4-2, 8-5-3a(1)*

**6.** **Four** players numbered 50–79 on the line (not five), and the snapper is an **ineligible** forward-pass receiver for that down unless the pass is touched by B. This first/second/third-down exception applies **only to the snapper**; the broader "any 0–49 or 80–99 may replace any 50–79" exception is limited to fourth down and kick tries.
*Rule 7-2-5b EXCEPTION 1*

**7.** If B **accepts** the penalty for A2's illegal touching by an ineligible, enforcement is from a spot in A's end zone — **safety, 2 points for B**. If B **declines**, it is **B's ball, first and goal from A's 8**. B will take the safety.
*Case 8.5.2 SIT D; Rules 5-1-3c, 7-5-13 PENALTY, 8-5-2c*

**8.** **The touchdown is cancelled.** The ball returns to the 50, **third and 10 for Team A**. (Contrast: had it actually been a *fifth* down run in error, the score is likewise cancelled but the ball goes to R, first and 10 at the 50.)
*Case 5.1.1 SIT D*

**9.** (a) A may keep the field goal and have the penalty enforced on the **first play of overtime**, which is the succeeding spot. (b) A may keep the touchdown but the penalty may **only** be enforced on the **try** — it cannot be carried into overtime, because there is no subsequent kickoff.
*Case 8.2.2 SIT C; Rule 8-2-2*

**10.** If K **accepts** R55's holding penalty, it is enforced from the **previous spot** — PSK enforcement cannot apply because K is next to put the ball in play as a result of the fumble recovery — and R's award at the spot of first touching is **cancelled**. If K **declines**, R takes the ball at the spot of first touching (R's 30).
*Case 6.2.5 SIT C; Rules 2-12-1, 2-12-2, 2-16-2h, 6-2-5*
