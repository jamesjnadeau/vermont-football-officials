Every signal, S1 through S47, in one place — the chart order, not grouped by
foul type. For the numbering system, who signals what, and how signals chain
together, see [Official Signals](/information/official-signals/). These are
our own stick-figure drawings of the *motions*; the NFHS chart's own artwork
is copyright Referee and is not reproduced here. Where a drawing and the
printed chart disagree, the chart wins — check it on pages 96–97 of your
rules book.
