Where youth flag parts company with Friday night: the flag rule first, then the high school one. 5/6 and 7/8 are the grade levels; HS is NFHS.

<p class="card-omit">This card is a summary and nothing more. The rules behind it are on <a href="/information/flag-football-rules/">Youth Flag Football Rules</a> and in the <a href="/information/football-rules-summary/">NFHS rules summary</a>, and your league's own sheet beats both of them where they disagree. "5/6" and "7/8" are the two grade levels flag is played at; "HS" is the NFHS rule the flag rule replaces. One thing neither page can answer: the flag rule set says nothing at all about overtime, so ask your commissioner before the night you need it.</p>

## Clock

- **Quarters run.** 10 min at 5/6, 12 at 7/8; halftime 5, no break between. HS: four 12s, stopped, 15 at half.
- **Stop-time** is the last minute of each half only, and none while a team leads by 18.
- **Play clock** 45 at 5/6, 30 at 7/8; first offence a warning. HS: 40/25, always 5.
- **Timeouts** two a half, one minute; a coach may call one. HS: three. An injury sits the player a play.
- **Kneel-down** is announced; the defense is told not to rush. No HS equivalent.

## Field, teams and who may run

- **Field** 40 × 80 yards, 7 to 11 a side, both teams equal. HS: 160ft × 100 yards, and 11.
- **Coaches on the field** all season at 5/6, first two games at 7/8. HS: never.
- **Weight rule:** over 120lbs (5/6) or 150 (7/8) may not run as a back or returner. They may play QB in a helmet cover — hand off or pass, never run.

## Scoring

- **A touchdown needs the ball *and* the flags** across the plane. HS: the ball alone. Same for a safety.
- **Try** 1 from the 2½, 2 from the 5, run or pass; no field goals. HS: one snap from the 3, kick for 1.
- **After a safety** the ball goes to the 40. HS: the team scored on free-kicks from its 20.

## Leading by 18 — the mercy rule

- **Mandatory, in this order**, one more each time the leader scores again: rushers wait 3 seconds; the trailing team gets 5 downs; the leader 3, and punts or surrenders on third.
- The clock then stops only for injuries and officials' timeouts. HS: a running clock is a state adoption, and never touches downs.

## Live ball, dead ball

- **The spot is where the flag came off**, not where the runner ended up.
- **Down** on a knee, on a fall with the flags on, when progress stops, or whenever the official judges the runner unsafe — wider than any HS rule.
- **Leaving the feet to advance** is illegal; spot where they left the ground. HS: legal, but hurdling an upright opponent is 15.
- **Stiff arms are legal.** A push out is legal only within 3 feet of the sideline; beyond that, a tackle.
- **Fumbles at 5/6 are dead** where they hit the ground, dropped snaps included. Live for either team at 7/8, as at HS.
- **A muffed kickoff at 7/8** is R's alone, and R may advance it. HS: R touching a free kick makes it K's to recover.

## Kicks, and the punt that isn't

- **Nobody punts.** The ball is moved 25 yards (5/6) or 30 (7/8), and never past the opponent's 20.
- **No kickoff at 5/6** — the game, the half and every score restart at the 20.
- **At 7/8 kick from the 30.** R's front line holds between its 40 and 35 until the kick: five minimum, four on a 7-man team, none over 150lbs behind.

## Alignment — count before every snap

- **Offense:** at most three off the line, the QB among them. Guard–centre–guard, splits of 1 to 3 feet, helmets inside the centre's waist.
- **Linemen downfield:** 2 yards in flag, 3 at HS. 5 yards either way.
- **Defense:** 3 on the line of 7, 4 of 8 or 9, 5 of 10. Linebackers 2 yards off. No nose tackle head-up on the centre. HS dictates none of it.
- **Only defensive linemen may rush**, never more than the line requires. HS: anyone, any number.
- **The 3-yard box:** the QB is a passer until they break it on a roll-out; contact before that is roughing.

## Penalties that read differently

- **Tackling is a foul, and graded:** incidental 5, intentional 10, flagrant 10 plus 10, repeated flagrant 20 and out four quarters.
- **Legal though the runner goes down:** a push out inside 3 feet, a blocker driven into the runner, impeding the runner to reach the flag.
- **Blocking** is open hands, nothing low or from behind at all. HS: linemen may do both in the free-blocking zone.
- **Face mask** 10 — from the spot on offense, from the line on defense. HS: 15 either way.
- **Pass interference** 10 from the line; inside the 10 or in the end zone, ball on the 1 and first down. HS: 15, and a first down on the defensive one.
- **The breakaway rule:** a runner held after obviously breaking loose gets the 1 and a first down. No HS equivalent.
- **Guarding the flag** costs the spot, no yardage. **Unsportsmanlike** is 10 from the line; two in a game sits the player four quarters.
- **Preventive officiating** is in this rule set by name. Warn first — these are 10- to 14-year-olds.
