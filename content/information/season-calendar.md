**This page covers the 2026 season.** If you are reading it in a later year,
every date below is wrong — go to the current VPA Football Guide on
[Athletic Guides & Rules](https://vpaonline.org/athletic-guides-rules/) instead.

This is the page that goes stale fastest on the site, and the first one to
update each August.

## The season at a glance

| Date | What |
| --- | --- |
| Monday **10 August** | First day of fall practice |
| Saturday **22 August** | First day interscholastic controlled scrimmages are allowed (practice day 13) |
| Friday **28** or Saturday **29 August** | Second allowed scrimmage day (practice days 19–20) |
| Weeks 1–8 | Regular season. Eight games maximum, scheduled by the VIFL |
| Friday **23 October**, noon | Deadline to withdraw from the tournament — noon the day before pairings |
| Saturday **24 October** | Playoff **pairings posted**, Divisions I, II and III |
| Week 9 | First round of the playoffs |
| **29, 30 or 31 October** | Championship quarterfinals, Divisions I and III |
| Friday **6** or Saturday **7 November** | Semifinals, Divisions I, II and III, hosted by the top seeds |
| Saturday **14 November** | **Finals, all divisions, at Burlington High School** |
| Saturday **14 November** | Last day of the fall season |

**Game dates within weeks 1 through 8 are not in the guide.** Schools play the
schedule assigned by the VIFL, and any change to it has to be approved by the
VPA and VIFL. Your assignments in Arbiter are the authority on when you are
working; see [Getting Assigned](/information/getting-assigned/).

## Preseason practice, and why officials care

Officials do not work practices, but the practice calendar tells you when
players will first have been in full pads and when the first live contact
happened — useful context for a week 1 game.

| Practice days | What is allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–2 (10–11 Aug) | Helmets only |
| Days 3–5 (12–14 Aug) | Helmets and shoulder pads with shorts |
| Day 6 (15 Aug) | First day of full protective equipment and body-to-body contact; first double session |
| Day 13 (22 Aug) | First allowed controlled scrimmage |
| Days 19–20 (28–29 Aug) | Second allowed scrimmage |

A player needs **seven practice sessions** before any interscholastic scrimmage
and **ten practices** before their first game.

## Playoff seeding

Seeds come from a quality point rating, not from win-loss record alone:

- 10 points for beating a Division I team, 7 for a Division II team, 5 for a
  Division III team — that is a team's own value.
- A team that loses to a higher-division opponent still earns the divisional
  differential: a D-II team earns 3 points for losing to a D-I team, a D-III
  team earns 2 for losing to a D-II team.
- 2 points for every victory earned by any opponent — the opponents' value.
- Own value plus opponents' value, divided by the number of VIFL games played,
  gives the QPR. Higher QPR seeds higher.
- Ties are broken head to head, then by the VPA fall tournament rules.

Semifinal pairings are #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3, at the higher seed's home field.

## Administrative deadlines

The VPA guide does not set the association's own dates. What is known from the
VFOA officers:

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Preseason meetings | Three or four, at least one in person. **Dates not yet published here.** |
| In-season meetings | Mostly virtual. **Dates not yet published here.** |
| Availability in Arbiter | No hard deadline, but have it in **a week before the season starts** |
| Registration fee ($25) and background check | At sign-up, through the registration system |
| Membership dues (~$100) | Waived the first year; usually taken from the mileage reimbursement after that |
| Mileage claim | Submitted on a form; reimbursed at the end of the season |
| Rules exam | There is none. Quizzes are used as a group activity. |

See [Becoming an Official](/information/becoming-an-official/) and
[Your First Season](/information/your-first-season/) for what those involve.

**The association's meeting dates are the gap on this page.** They are the thing
a first-year official most wants from a calendar, and they are not published
anywhere this site can read. If you have them, they belong here — the
[source register](https://github.com/jamesjnadeau/vermont-football-officials/blob/master/docs/sources.md)
tracks it as an open item.
